Features

Less admin. More huddle.

Build next week in minutes, not hours.

Start from last week, a template, or a blank board. Huddlebee flags gaps and overtime before you publish.

  • Reusable templates for busy and quiet weeks
  • Overtime and coverage warnings
  • Publish to everyone's phone in one tap

Swaps without the group chat chaos.

Someone can't make Thursday? They offer the shift, a teammate claims it, and you approve with one tap.

  • Swap and drop requests in the app
  • Only qualified people can claim a shift
  • Manager approval, or auto-approve rules

Reminders that cut no-shows in half.

Friendly nudges the night before and an hour ahead, by app notification or text, in each person's language.

  • Automatic reminders by app or SMS
  • Clock-in from a phone, with location check
  • No-show alerts sent to managers
