❓ Good questions

Before your first visit

What does my dog need to join? +
Up-to-date vaccines (rabies, DHPP and bordetella), a friendly temperament, and a free trial day so we can match them with the right play group.
How do you keep the play groups safe? +
Dogs play in small groups sorted by size and energy level, always with a certified handler. Rest breaks are built into every day.
Can I watch my dog during the day? +
Yes! Every yard has a live webcam you can open from your phone, plus you'll get a report card with photos at pick-up.
What if I need to cancel? +
Cancel up to 24 hours ahead at no charge. Same-day cancellations are charged at half price.

Your first day is on us!

Bring your pup for a free trial day. We'll learn their personality and send you home with a report card and a very sleepy dog.
