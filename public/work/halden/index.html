    02 — Studio

    We design the things you notice least, and feel most.

    Good rooms don't ask for attention. They hold the morning light a little longer, they stay cool in August, and they still feel right in twenty years. That is the work: removing until only what matters is left.

    We are a team of nine architects and interior designers. We take on a small number of projects each year so every one gets a partner's full attention, from the first sketch to the last switch plate.

    01

    Light first

    Every plan begins with the sun's path across the site. Windows are placed for the hours you'll actually use a room.

    02

    Honest materials

    Concrete, timber, lime plaster, stone. Materials that age well and are allowed to look like what they are.

    03

    Nothing extra

    If a detail doesn't serve the people using the space, it doesn't make it into the drawings.

    03 — Process

    Four stages, one team, start to finish.

    1. 01

      Listen

      A walk through your site or space and a long conversation about how you live and work.

      2–3 weeks
    2. 02

      Sketch

      Hand sketches and simple models of two or three directions, refined together into one.

      4–6 weeks
    3. 03

      Detail

      Full drawings, materials and specifications, priced with trusted builders before anything is built.

      8–12 weeks
    4. 04

      Build

      We stay on site through construction, solving problems early and protecting the design.

      6–14 months
